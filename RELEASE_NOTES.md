## DocScanner v0.5.52

- ✏️ New **Edit Text** with DS AI, working like CamScanner's:
  - Opens with **DS AI is analyzing** and a scanning light sweeping over the page
  - Tap any line of text to get **Copy, Delete, Select All** and the DS AI tools **Edit Text, AI Proofread, AI Polish**
  - **Edit Text** sheet: "DS AI matches your text to the original font style." with Cancel / Confirm
  - **DS AI is processing** while the line is redrawn in the original ink colour, size and position, then **Processing complete**
  - Undo / redo, live character count, and nothing is changed until you tap **Save** ("Changes can't be undone once saved.", with "Don't ask again for 7 days")
  - After saving, the page asks **Are you satisfied with the text editing?**
- AI Proofread and AI Polish use the AI provider you set up in Me → AI Mode and ask before sending any text
