## DocScanner v0.5.60

- 🖼️ Scan → Import Images: after you tap Import(n), the new document's pages appear right away, each showing "Processing images… Please do NOT quit the app" until it is cropped and enhanced, like CamScanner. Then the document opens (one photo goes straight to the edit preview, several photos to the page grid)
- ✅ Auto Crop is now tested on an Android emulator with nine photo situations users reported (paper on denim and patterned cloth, a page that runs off the photo edge, a white page on a light table, a creased page, a small receipt, an ID card with a dark top, a white card on dark cloth, a photo print, a bank card on pink cloth). All are found with at least 98.9% overlap with the real page edges
