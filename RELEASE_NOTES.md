## DocScanner v0.5.45

- 🌓 Camera scans no longer keep the document's shadow: when the outline follows a cast shadow, the crop is pulled onto the card or page itself, so no strip of table or cloth remains along the edges
- 📐 True-shape perspective correction: pages and cards photographed at an angle now come out in their real proportions (A4 stays A4, bank and ID cards keep the card shape)
